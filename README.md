# The Cottage

A cozy pixel-art cooking game. Grandma's kitchen deep in a snowy forest: pick a recipe from the old leather cookbook, gather ingredients from the pantry and icebox, and cook it step by step by the fire. Finished dishes go on the shelf. The cat sleeps on the rug.

Open `index.html` (or serve the folder). There is no build step and no network access.

- Recipes: apple pie, pumpkin pie, pecan pie, country bread, roast turkey, glazed ham, winter stew, cinnamon rolls, gingerbread, hot cocoa.
- Debug URLs: `?scene=kitchen`, `?scene=book`, `?cook=<id>&step=<n>`, `?surprise=deer|owl|cardinal|aurora|carolers|star`, `?fresh` (clears the save).
- Tests: `node tests/run-all.js`
- Music: two calm CC0 solo-piano pieces ("JRPG Piano" by Joth, "Soft Piano Emotional 1" by Tozan), played softly under the sound effects; the speaker icon mutes it.
